TanJin Converter

Converts manhwa into 320×480 pages for your TanJin reader. Everything happens on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Use 1 for a new series. When adding more chapters to a series already on the reader, enter the next number (for example 6 if the reader has chapters 1 to 5), or the new chapters will overwrite the old ones.

Saved as the series cover. You can also set a cover on the reader: double-tap, then Set as cover.

Pick one or more .zip / .cbz files (each becomes one chapter), or the images of a single chapter. For a whole series in one go, pick a single .zip that has one folder per chapter; each folder becomes a chapter. On a phone, very large series may need splitting into a few zips.

If the download didn’t start, tap here.

Easiest: open the TanJin menu, tap Wi-Fi, join its hotspot with your phone, and upload this ZIP at 192.168.4.1. Or unzip it and copy the series folder onto the SD card (keep the folder structure).

Books and stories (EPUB or text)

Only convert books you own or have permission to use. Copy-protected (DRM) EPUBs can’t be converted.

EPUBs usually carry their own cover and it is used automatically. A cover you pick here replaces it.

Use 1 for a new book. Text size is fixed when you convert; to change it, convert again. Result goes in a Books folder on the reader.

How the reader will see it
  1. Images resized to 320 px wide
  2. Joined into one strip per chapter, cut into pages up to 480 px tall
  3. Saved as standard JPG: Series/Chapter_001/001.jpg
